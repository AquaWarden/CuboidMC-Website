Hypixel Housing

The Backrooms Experience | 2.0

About The Project

Orginally a small independent project, The Backrooms Experience 2.0 is a Hypixel Housing bringing the horrifying BACKROOMS to Minecraft.

Key Features

• Custom-built liminal environments capturing the uncanny feel of Level 0 and beyond.

• Interactive housing mechanics and tailored player navigation system.

• Tons of levels to explore.

• Mysterious entities who find you curious.

Platform & Availability

This experience was hosted on the Hypixel Network via Hypixel Housing.

How to join

You can join this housing using the following steps:

1. Make sure you are on Java edition

2. Click on the Multiplayer button then click "Add Server"

3. In the text box under "Server Name", write whatever. In the text box under "Server IP", write "mc.hypixel.net".

4. Join the server, then navigate and right click the compass.

5. Click on the dark oak door labeled, "Housing".

6. In the commands menu, do "/visit AquaWarden", then click on the gold ingot.

You can now play!
