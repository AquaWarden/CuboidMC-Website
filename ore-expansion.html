Ore Expansion Mod

Project Overview

Ore Expansion is a Minecraft Mod in active development that adds new ores, tools, and more to the game. It makes the player want to strongly progress through the game and enjoy more of Minecraft with friends, for these new ores can be rare and common, but it adds more fun nevertheless.

What to Expect

• Tons of New Ores: Discover rare metals and magical crystals deep within underground caverns.

• Expanded Crafting Trees: Use newly mined resources to forge custom weapons, tools, and armor sets.

• Unique World Generation: Custom vein spawning algorithms to encourage deep cave exploration.

Development Status

This mod is currently in active creation. Stay tuned for sneak peeks, beta testing announcements, and release details!
